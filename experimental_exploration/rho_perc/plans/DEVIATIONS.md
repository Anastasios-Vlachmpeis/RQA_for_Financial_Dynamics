# Deviations

| Date | Phase | What | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-14 | P0 | In `rhoperc.core.rho_perc`, the bisection bracket [ε_iso, ε_lag] is used only for the 8-neighbourhood; for the 4-neighbourhood the search extends to the largest distance in Ω. The bounds ρ_iso/ρ_lag are still computed and reported unchanged, and K4 is checked on the 8-neighbourhood. | A single diagonal at lag ℓ is a chain of diagonal steps, which is 8-connected but not 4-connected, so ε_lag is not an upper bound for the 4-neighbourhood. Without this, fast and reference disagree on 4-neighbourhood cases (K3). The Article 3 definition is unchanged. |
| 2026-11-14 | P0 | In `rhoperc.selfcheck`, the "half with distances rounded to create ties" cases (K3–K6) use series on an integer grid (values 0–9), so tied distances are exact in floating point. | Rounding a continuous series to 1 decimal creates distances that are mathematically tied but differ by ~1e-16 in floating point; a strictly increasing transform such as log(1+D) can then split them, so K5 could not hold exactly. The constitution's requirement (exact ties, fast = reference, monotone invariance) is preserved. |
| 2026-11-14 | P3 | Decision T is evaluated with mean **P3c** power. | The constitution's Decision T wording says "mean P3d power", but P3 has parts a–c only (no P3d). Interpreted as P3c (the power part) and pre-registered in the P3 spec before any run; the constitution text is not renamed. |
| 2026-11-14 | P4 | Spec version bumped 1 -> 2 before any full result (smoke v1 failed on plumbing), then 2 -> 3 for a labelling fix only. Sampling step is varied via `sample_every` at fixed integrator `dt` (F2 x0.5 via dt=0.005), not via `dt*f`; smoke sampling factors are [0.5, 2.0] only and smoke caches live in `smoke_v<version>/`. In v3 the baseline x1 sampling rows carry their `fixed_N`/`fixed_span` mode in the output tables (grid, seeds and values unchanged). | Multiplying `dt` by 4 made the Rössler RK4 integration overflow, and fixed-span x4 at the smoke base (N=100 states) leaves fewer than 20 states so the Theiler rule can exceed N/2. The sampled time steps themselves are unchanged. Pre-registered in the v2/v3 specs before their full runs. |
