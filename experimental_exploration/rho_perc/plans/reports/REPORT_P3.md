# REPORT P3 — known transitions and power

**What was run.** P3a: logistic map, r = 3.5–4.0 in steps of 0.001 (501 values), N = 1000 states, m ∈ {1, 3}, transforms {z, rank}; per r: ρ_perc, ρ_iso, ρ_lag, and DET, LAM, L from `recplay.rqa.rqa` at RR = 10 %, plus Lyapunov λ = mean of ln|r(1 − 2x)| over 100 000 iterates (x₀ = 0.5, 1000 transient iterates discarded) — 2004 rows in `notebooks/tables/rho_perc/P3/logistic.csv`, scores in `logistic_scores.csv`. P3b: N = 400, 100 runs per cell, `d_lin` with 19 IAAFT surrogates plus raw values — permanent break (S3 + midpoint shift of 0, 0.5, 1, 2, 4 process sd, process sd = 1 as for X2) and recurring regimes (AR(1) 0.8, mean switching with stay probability 0.98, gap 0, 1, 2, 4 sd) — 1800 rows in `breaks.csv`, trends in `breaks_summary.csv`, cell medians in `breaks_cell_summary.csv`. P3c: D1, D3, F1 at observational noise 0, 0.25, 0.5, 1.0 sd plus S5, S6 at noise 0; N = 400, 60 runs × 39 IAAFT surrogates, both transforms; ρ_perc, DET and LAM each tested against the same surrogates — 1680 rows in `power.csv`, rejection rates in `power_summary.csv`. All gate and Decision-T evaluation choices (below) were written in `spec.json` before any outcome was computed. Seeds are `default_rng([3, cell, rep])`; one heavy job ran at a time; total run time about 14 minutes.

**Gate G3: PASS.** Computed by code in `notebooks/tables/rho_perc/P3/decision.csv`; in fact all three parts pass. (a) Logistic periodic-vs-chaotic AUC for ρ_perc at m = 1 is 0.962 (z) and 0.909 (rank) — either transform counting was pre-registered, and both are ≥ 0.90. (b) Permanent-break Spearman (size vs raw ρ_perc, pooled runs) is 0.544 (z) and 0.534 (rank), both ≥ 0.5. (c) With a cell defined before the run as process × noise × transform (28 cells) and statistic ρ_perc, 6 cells reject in ≥ 50 % of runs (D3 at noise 0, 0.25, 0.5 under both transforms in varying combination, and S6 under rank), ≥ the required 3. No STOP file.

**Decision T: keep `z`.** Also in `decision.csv`, computed by code. Rank's m = 1 ρ_perc AUC (0.909) is more than 0.03 below z's (0.962), so rank fails the first condition even though its mean P3c ρ_perc power (0.296 over all 28 cells) is within 0.03 of z's (0.273). P4–P8 therefore use the `z` transform only.

**Findings** (numbers from the named CSVs):

1. P3a, which measure is best: at the primary m = 1 under z, the best periodic-vs-chaotic AUC overall is L (0.997, `logistic_scores.csv`), then ρ_lag (0.988). Of the three measures the phase compares, **ρ_perc is best** (AUC 0.962), ahead of DET (0.951) and LAM (0.876); ρ_perc also tracks λ over chaotic r more strongly (Spearman 0.810) than LAM (0.491) or DET (−0.366), though L (−0.957) and ρ_lag (0.859) track it more strongly still. λ was never exactly 0, so no r was excluded (n_lambda_zero_excluded = 0; 114 periodic, 387 chaotic r values).
2. The flagged transition windows in `logistic.csv` (column `transition_window`, ±0.01) are confirmed by λ, not assumed: in the period-3 window around 3.8284, λ goes strongly negative (minimum −1.329; half of the window's r values have λ < 0); around the interior crisis 3.8568 λ is mostly positive (median 0.211) with a quarter of values slightly negative (minimum −0.062); around band merging 3.6786 λ stays positive on 95 % of the window (median 0.336, minimum −0.033), i.e. chaotic on both sides, as expected for a merging of chaotic bands.
3. P3b, permanent break: the stated expectation holds for raw ρ_perc (Spearman 0.544 z / 0.534 rank, `breaks_summary.csv`; median ρ_perc under z rises from 0.422 at size 0 to 0.630 at 4 sd, `breaks_cell_summary.csv`), and more strongly for `d_lin` (0.657 z). But of the three compared measures **DET is best** here (Spearman 0.836 z), with LAM close (0.832); ρ_perc is the weakest of the three on this part.
4. P3b, recurring regimes: raw ρ_perc under z shows no trend with the gap (Spearman −0.019, p = 0.70), confirming the Article 2 F8 pattern for ρ_perc itself, while under rank there is a weak rise (0.286). DET and LAM, by contrast, rise strongly with the gap (0.875 and 0.869 under z). So "regimes do not link up late" is a statement about ρ_perc, not about recurrence measures in general.
5. P3c, power: **DET is best, LAM second, ρ_perc last.** Mean rejection rates over all cells (`power_summary.csv`) are, under z, DET 0.611, LAM 0.449, ρ_perc 0.273 (rank: 0.586, 0.489, 0.296). ρ_perc reaches ≥ 0.5 only for D3 (Hénon) at low noise and S6 under rank; for D1 its rejection rate is 0.08–0.22 at all noise levels while DET is at 0.98–1.00 up to noise 0.5. The G2 limits carry over: these are the same IAAFT tests that were oversized in four z-transform null cells in P2.
6. In all saved P3 runs, ρ_iso ≤ ρ_perc ≤ ρ_lag held with 0 violations (`logistic.csv`, `breaks.csv`, `power.csv`).

**Deviations.** One, recorded in `plans/rho_perc/DEVIATIONS.md`: the constitution's Decision T says "mean P3d power" but P3 has parts a–c only, so mean P3c power was used; this was pre-registered in `spec.json` before the run and the constitution text was not renamed. No STOP file was written. Smoke runs were used for plumbing and timing only; no smoke number appears here.

**What it means for the paper.** ρ_perc does respond to known structure — it separates periodic from chaotic logistic regimes almost as well as the best standard measures and rises after a permanent break while staying flat across recurring regimes under z — but on breaks and on IAAFT power it is beaten by DET (and usually LAM), so the paper may claim it as a complement with a specific permanent-break/regime contrast, not as a stronger detector than DET. Later phases use the z transform only (Decision T).
